Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Home Inventory & Expiry ("the App"), developed by Clyde Stefan Dias, handles information when you use the App.

1. Overview

Home Inventory & Expiry is an offline-first Android application for managing household inventory and tracking expiry dates.

The App is designed to keep your information on your Android device. It does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization.

2. Information Stored by the App

The App allows you to create and manage inventory information such as:

  • Item names
  • User-created categories
  • Manufactured dates
  • Expiry dates
  • Primary and alternate home locations
  • Descriptions and notes
  • Photos attached to inventory items

This information is stored locally on your Android device for the functionality of the App.

3. Photos

If you choose to add photos, the App may use Android's system photo selection functionality or the device camera.

Photos selected or captured for inventory items are copied into the App's private application storage and are used only for the inventory functionality.

The App does not upload your inventory photos to a cloud service or transmit them to the developer.

4. Notifications

The App may use local Android notifications to remind you about approaching expiry dates.

Notification processing is performed locally on your device. The App does not send notification information to a remote server.

On supported Android versions, notification permission is requested from you before notifications are used.

5. Data Collection and Sharing

The App does not collect or transmit your inventory information, photos, or notes to the developer or to third-party servers.

The App does not provide user accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or third-party advertising SDKs.

The App does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your locally stored inventory information with third parties.

6. Internet and Network Access

The App is designed to operate without network access. It does not require an internet connection for its inventory, photo, search, expiry tracking, or notification functionality.

7. Backup and Device Transfer

The App explicitly excludes its local inventory database, private inventory photos, and notification state from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup configurations.

As a result, your inventory data and App-owned photos may not be restored automatically when the App is installed on another device.

8. Security

The App stores its local data within Android's application-private storage environment and uses Android platform mechanisms for accessing photos and camera content.

The App's database is not protected by application-level database encryption. The security of information stored on the device therefore also depends on the security of the Android device itself, including its screen lock, operating system security, and physical access controls.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete inventory items and their associated information from within the App.

When an inventory item is deleted, the App removes its database record and cleans up App-owned image files that are no longer referenced by any inventory item.

Uninstalling the App removes its application data according to Android's standard application data removal behavior.

10. Permissions

The App may request Android permissions or system access needed for specific functionality, including notification permission on supported Android versions and access provided through Android's system photo picker and camera functionality.

The App does not require location access, contacts access, microphone access, or an account sign-in.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose household inventory application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through remote services because the App does not transmit user information to remote services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
